# MobileLab Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MobileLab is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, socioeconomic status, or technology choices.

Participation in this project is a privilege accompanied by responsibility. We expect everyone interacting in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and other project spaces to help create an environment where people can collaborate without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the MobileLab community include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect for different experiences and viewpoints.
- Giving and receiving constructive technical feedback without making it personal.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting stated boundaries and the privacy of other community members.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what benefits the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Bad-faith participation, including impersonation or knowingly misleading others.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its review.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every official MobileLab project space. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project, such as by using an official project account, speaking on behalf of MobileLab, or participating in an event as a project representative.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through the repository's [security advisory workflow](https://github.com/GianSandoval5/MobileLab/security/advisories/new) and identify the report as a code-of-conduct concern. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Project maintainers will acknowledge reports as promptly as practical, review them fairly, and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved to the greatest extent possible. Anyone handling a report must disclose a conflict of interest and step aside when necessary. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this code; an unsubstantiated good-faith report does not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. Depending on the context, severity, and history of the behavior, maintainers may apply:

1. **Correction:** A private explanation of the impact and a request to correct the behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with clear expectations and consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A time-limited suspension from project interactions or representation.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Removal from the community and its project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will prioritize community safety, proportionality, consistency, and opportunities for repair when appropriate. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from their project responsibilities.
